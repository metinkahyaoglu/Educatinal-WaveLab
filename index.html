Constructive interferenceThe waves meet in phase (0°, 360°) and reinforce each other. Physical: pressure 2x = +6 dB. Perception: clearly louder.
Partial interferencePhysical: 1.41x = +3 dB at 90°, 1x = 0 dB at 120° (same as one wave), below 1x between 120° and 180°. Perception: from a noticeable increase to a noticeable decrease.
Destructive interferenceThe waves meet in antiphase (180°) and cancel each other. Physical: in an ideal medium the pressures cancel out completely, total 0x. Perception: no sound is heard. In a real room, reflections prevent total cancellation and leave a deep dip.
